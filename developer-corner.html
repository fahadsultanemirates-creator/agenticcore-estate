03

A dashboard built for developers

The Developer Dashboard is completely separate from the standard buyer/seller dashboard — no referral tree, no points ledger. Just your active package, your listing quota, and what you've already posted, tracked as one clear account status card.

The Developer Corner dashboard also carries a reserved slot for the AI Document Check tool, for when document review is switched back on later.
