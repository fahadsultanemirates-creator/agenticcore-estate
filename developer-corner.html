Verification comes first

Every developer account submits a government ID (CNIC) and a company or registration document before anything goes live. A human reviewer checks both in our admin panel — normally within 72 hours.

03

A dashboard built for developers

Once approved, the Developer Dashboard is separate from the standard buyer/seller dashboard — it tracks your tier, listing count, verification status, and (soon) your Growth Score for every listing.

The Developer Corner dashboard also carries a reserved slot for the AI Document Check tool, so re-verifications and future document updates will eventually be pre-screened automatically.
