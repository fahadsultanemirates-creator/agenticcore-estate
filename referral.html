Five levels, decreasing rewards

When someone you referred — or someone in their downline, up to five levels out — completes a transaction, points are paid up the entire chain.

L1

Someone you referred directly

Earn 10% of their transaction value in points.

L2

Someone they referred

Earn 5% of that transaction value.

L3

Third level out

Earn 3% of that transaction value.

L4

Fourth level out

Earn 2% of that transaction value.

L5

Fifth level out

Earn 1% of that transaction value.

A worked example

Scenario

You refer Sara. Sara refers Ali, who refers Nadia, who refers Zain, who refers Rabia.

Rabia's referral, a Developer Corner account, closes a listing transaction worth Rs 500,000 in fees.

What happens

Zain (L1 from Rabia) earns Rs 50,000 in points.

Nadia (L2) earns Rs 25,000. Ali (L3) earns Rs 15,000. Sara (L4) earns Rs 10,000. You (L5) earn Rs 5,000.
